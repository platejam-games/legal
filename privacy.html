Plate Jam — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Plate Jam (the “Game”) uses, why, and what choices you have. In short: the Game has no Plate Jam accounts, and we do not build profiles of you or sell your data for money. Since the November 2026 update the Game shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional reward videos that you choose to watch; Google may personalise the ads only for players aged 16 or over, and in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland only with your consent (section 4b). Your game progress is saved on your phone and, if your phone is signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or uses iCloud (iPhone), a copy is also kept in your own Google Play Games or iCloud account so it comes back on a new phone (section 2). Apart from that copy, the data that leaves the device is what is needed to process and restore in-app purchases, to download the current offers and bonus events (section 3), to show the ads (section 4b) and — only if you allow it — anonymous usage statistics that help us improve the Game (section 4). You can change these choices at any time in Settings › Anonymous statistics and, where it applies, Settings › Ad privacy choices.

1. Who is responsible (data controller)

2. Data stored on your device and in your cloud save

The following is saved locally on your phone (app storage). It is never sent to us; it is only copied to your own Google Play Games or iCloud account, as described below under “Cloud save”:

  • Game progress: levels and stars, coins, gems, items, chefs, decorations, Kitchen Pass progress, settings (e.g. sound on/off) and a local copy of the purchases already delivered to you, so they are not delivered twice.
  • Local gameplay statistics: a small log of level starts, wins, fails and times used only to show statistics inside the Game. It is not transmitted.

Sound, music, vibration (haptics) and the optional game reminders (local notifications, only if you allow them) run entirely on the device; the reminders are scheduled on the phone, without any push service. The Game does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or other apps. Your chef name (the name on the Home screen, chosen by you, no real name needed) is stored on the device and, with the cloud save, in your own Google Play Games or iCloud account; we do not receive it.

Cloud save (Google Play Games / iCloud)

To bring your progress back on a new phone or after a reinstall, the Game automatically copies the whole game save — levels, stars, coins, gems, items, chefs, decorations, Kitchen Pass, your chef name, settings and the local record of purchases already delivered — together with a random device identifier created by the Game and the time of the save, to your own platform account. No Plate Jam account is needed:

  • Android: to Google Play Games Services “Saved Games”, when your phone is signed in to Google Play Games. Google stores the copy in your Google account, in a hidden app-data area of your Google Drive.
  • iPhone: to iCloud key-value storage in your Apple account, when iCloud is on for the Game.

If two phones have different progress, the Game asks you which one to keep. Moving progress between Android and iPhone is not supported. We never receive this copy: Google and Apple store it as independent controllers under your account and their own privacy policies (section 5). Legal basis: performance of the contract, i.e. providing the save and restore feature (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). To turn it off: on Android, don’t sign in to Google Play Games, or sign out in the Play Games app (the Game’s Settings then show “Cloud save: off”); on iPhone, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud and turn off Plate Jam. To delete the copy, see section 8.

“Challenge a friend” and sharing: only when you tap “Challenge a friend” or “Share”, the Game prepares a message for your phone’s share sheet. A challenge link contains the level, your score and your chef name; you decide whether and to whom to send it (through the app you pick, under that app’s own terms). We do not receive the link. If Plate Jam is installed, the link can open the Game directly; otherwise it shows a static page on GitHub Pages (platejam-games.github.io), which, like any web host, may process the visitor’s IP address in its server logs (GitHub’s privacy statement applies). The level, score and chef name are written after the “#” in the link, so they stay in the visitor’s browser and are not sent to GitHub; the page uses no cookies or trackers. A chef name containing offensive words is refused in the Game and not shown to the person who opens the link.

Ads start only after you have won level 10 of the Food Truck and answered the year-of-birth question (below). Before that the Game does not start Google’s ads service and makes no ad request.

Some laws (for example in Texas and other U.S. states, and in Brazil) require apps to take into account the age range that the app store has for the user.   • Android: only on phones from the United States or Brazil, the Game asks Google Play Age Signals for the age range of your Google Play account; Google Play may show its own prompt. To know this, the Game reads the country on the device (from the SIM card, the mobile network or the region settings), without any permission. Everywhere else the Game never contacts this service.

Puedes cambiar estas decisiones en cualquier momento en Ajustes › Estadísticas anónimas y, cuando corresponda, en Ajustes › Privacidad de los anuncios.

Edad
